Examples
1. Creating VLANs

vlan 10
name Faculty/Staff 

Repeats with all vlans


VLANs Verification

sh int vlan


2. Assign VLANs to active ports

int f0/11
switchport mode access
switchport access vlan 10
no shut


Cisco VLAN Voice Traffic

int f0/11
mls qos trust cos
switchport voice vlan 150


Connectivity Verification

sh vlan brief
